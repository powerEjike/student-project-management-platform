# Student feedback email drafts

The current primary supervisor opens an active approved project and its exact reviewed PDF or Word file, then chooses **Prepare feedback attachment** in Document review. At least one saved passage remark is required. Archived, defence-cleared, completed, pending-release and withheld projects are excluded, matching the deadline-reminder eligibility policy. Co-supervisors, HODs and Super Admins cannot use this draft tool. Ordinary authorized annotated-PDF export remains available separately.

ProjectTrack reads current student, supervisor, department and saved feedback records from the server. It builds the same annotated PDF used by the existing export, including its native comments, review summary and recorded feedback responses. For Word, the attachment uses the locally converted review pages and retains the existing conversion notice; it is not the original Word file. Digital-signature and review-engine restrictions still apply. Contact logs, release reasons and integrity case records are not included. The original document is never uploaded by this workflow.

Download the attachment to review it. Check the student address and edit the message if needed. A missing roster address can be entered locally; edits do not update the roster. Confirm that the attachment, recipient and wording have been reviewed, then download the `.eml` draft. Both attachment download and draft download recheck access and compare the current student/project/feedback snapshot. A changed snapshot requires rebuilding and reviewing a fresh attachment. The check is a read snapshot rather than a lock on later changes.

Open the draft in a compatible email app, verify the attachment, recipient and sending account, then send manually. No sender, CC or BCC is preset. Email-app support and provider limits vary. Attachments are limited to 15 MiB before base64 encoding; use the separate PDF export and your email app for larger files. The MIME draft contains a UTF-8 message and one PDF attachment with a fixed safe filename.

Preparation and download do not send email, create a delivery log, record successful contact or prove receipt. Record actual contact outcomes separately after sending. No provider credentials, background email worker, paid plan, Firestore writes or new access rules are introduced. Keep downloaded drafts private: they contain the student's address and reviewed work.
